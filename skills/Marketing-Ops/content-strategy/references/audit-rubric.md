# Audit rubric

Scores an existing content program against the seven parts of the Orbit Media
framework (Andy Crestodina,
https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/content-strategy-framework/). The adoption
percentages in the 1% test are those cited in the source article; each carries
its original source so it can be re-checked. Own words throughout.

## How to score

For each part, ask for evidence (URLs, examples, numbers), then pick the row
that matches what you can actually see. Quote the evidence in the scorecard: a
URL, a number, or the user's own sentence in quotation marks, and say which
kind it is so the reader knows what was verified and what was reported.
If the user can't produce evidence, that's "missing" unless they can describe
something specific enough to verify later; then it's "partial, unverified".

## Rubric

### 1. Sales page core

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Present | A dedicated service page targets a named commercial-intent phrase within realistic reach of the site's DA; answers the top buyer questions; carries evidence next to claims; has CTAs top and bottom. |
| Partial | Page exists but the phrase is unclear or out of reach, or evidence and objection-handling are thin, or CTAs are generic. |
| Missing | No dedicated service page, or a brochure paragraph with a contact link. |

Evidence: URL, target phrase, DA, ranking pages' DA, conversion rate by landing-page type.

### 2. Mission statement

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Present | Written down, names audience + topics + benefit, visible on the blog and in signup CTAs, and recent content fits it. |
| Partial | Exists in a doc somewhere, or is implied, or recent content wanders outside it. |
| Missing | Nothing written. |

Evidence: the sentence, where it's published, three recent titles to test fit.
If the sentence exists and is published but titles weren't checked, score
Present and note "fit unverified". If it exists only in a document nobody sees,
Partial.

### 3. Original research

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Present | Published original data in the last 12 months, with method note, that has earned links. |
| Partial | Older research, or research with no links, or a survey run but never published. |
| Missing | None. |

Evidence: URL, publish date, referring domains to it.

### 4. Prospect questions

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Present | Articles traceable to real sales questions; sales sends them to prospects; a process exists for capturing new questions. |
| Partial | Some articles answer buyer questions by accident; sales doesn't use them. |
| Missing | Content is unrelated to what prospects ask. |

Evidence: three prospect questions and the article for each; how sales uses them.

### 5. Visuals

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Present | Original charts, diagrams, or video in the top pieces; research has a visual version. |
| Partial | Stock images only, or one infographic years ago. |
| Missing | Text only. |

Evidence: top three posts, count of original visuals in each.

### 6. Influencer collaboration

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Present | In the last quarter, at least one of: contributor quotes, round-up, interview, hosted guest, pitched guest. A named list of people exists. |
| Partial | Occasional mention or quote without outreach; no list. |
| Missing | Single-voice content only. |

Evidence: last quarter's collaborative pieces, the list.

### 7. Guest posting / PR

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Present | Pitched and published elsewhere in the last quarter, with links back; hosts guests. |
| Partial | Occasional bylines, or only press releases, or links only in bios. |
| Missing | Everything published at home. |

Evidence: URLs of external pieces in the last two quarters, link location.

## The 1% test

Four rare practices. Mark each true or false from the scores above (present
counts; partial doesn't).

| Practice | Source-cited adoption | True here? |
|---|---|---|
| Documented content mission (part 2 present) | 39% of B2B programs (CMI, https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/articles/research-b2b-audience/) | |
| Anchored in original research (part 3 present) | ~25% in the source's math; 49% of bloggers in Orbit's 2025 survey (https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/blogging-statistics/) | |
| Ongoing influencer collaboration (part 6 present) | 18% (Traackr / Altimeter, as cited in the source article) | |
| PR-focused / guest posting (part 7 present) | 60% in the source's math; 37% of bloggers pitch guest posts (Orbit survey) | |

Report "N of 4". The source article multiplies its four percentages to about
1% of programs doing all four. Say that, and say which ones are missing here.

## Ranking the gaps

Default order by link-and-authority impact:

1. Original research (part 3)
2. Guest posting / PR (part 7)
3. Influencer collaboration (part 6)
4. Sales page fixes (part 1). It jumps to first if the page converts at zero,
   or if conversion is unknown and the page scored Missing: no amount of
   authority helps a page that can't convert. If conversion is unknown and the
   page scored Partial, keep this position and make "measure it" the first line
   of the fix.
5. Mission (part 2)
6. Prospect questions (part 4), unless sales is starving for material, then
   move it up: it's the fastest payoff
7. Visuals (part 5)

Then adjust for capacity: what can this team realistically do this quarter?
State the adjustment and why.
