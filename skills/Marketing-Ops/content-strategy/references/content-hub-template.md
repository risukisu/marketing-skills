# The quarterly content hub

Distilled from Andy Crestodina / Orbit Media, "Connecting the dots" and "How
long will this take" in
https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/content-strategy-framework/. Own words
throughout. The twelve-slot structure follows the source; the calendar and
measurement plan are this skill's additions.

## What a hub is

One quarter's content, planned as a connected cluster instead of a list of
posts. Every piece points at the research and, where it fits, at the sales
page. Guest pieces point back in. The cluster earns links as a unit, and each
new hub raises the floor for the next one.

## Twelve-slot skeleton

| # | Piece | Purpose | Links to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales page (fixed up per part 1) | Rank, convert | Research (as proof) |
| 2 | Original research (part 3) | Links, credibility | Sales page, gated upgrade |
| 3 | Gated content upgrade: deeper version of the research | Pipeline (subscribers) | Research |
| 4 | Keyphrase how-to article, cites the research, quotes a contributor | Rank, pipeline | Research, sales page |
| 5 | Evil-twin version of #4, pitched as a guest post | Links | Research, sales page (bio) |
| 6 | Infographic from the research, with a short article | Links, reach | Research |
| 7 | Infographic plus short original article, pitched to a second site | Links | Research |
| 8 | Influencer interview on the research topic | Credibility, reach, links | Research |
| 9 | Podcast or contribution pitch: you, discussing the research | Links (show notes), credibility | Research |
| 10 | Blog post: a finding from the research, expanded | Rank, mission | Research |
| 11 | Blog post: a prospect question answered (part 4) | Pipeline | Sales page |
| 12 | Blog post: a second finding or a stance piece | Mission, reach | Research |

Adjust the mix to the company, but keep the shape: one research anchor, one
gated upgrade, at least two pieces that live on other sites, at least one
collaboration, and every piece with a stated purpose.

## When there is no research anchor yet

If step 3 ended without a research idea the team can run this quarter, don't
fake one and don't leave eight slots empty. Restructure Quarter 1 as a
foundation hub:

- Slot 2 becomes "design the research": pick the question, the data route,
  the list, the contributors. Fieldwork starts in week 9 so the study anchors
  Quarter 2.
- Slots 3, 6, 7, 10, 12 anchor on the best how-to article and the prospect
  questions instead: the gated upgrade is a checklist or calculator derived
  from a prospect question; the infographic is a process diagram; the guest
  pieces are the evil twin and a stance piece.
- Slots 8 and 9 still run; the interview and podcast pitch discuss the topic
  the research will measure, which also recruits respondents.
- Mark each substituted slot "anchor: research from Q2" so the plan is honest
  about what it's waiting for.

## Twelve-week calendar

| Weeks | Work |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Fix the sales page (#1). Design and field the research (#2). Line up contributors and interview subject. Send first guest-post pitches with titles. |
| 5-8 | Publish research, upgrade, how-to, infographic (#2, #3, #4, #6). Notify contributors. Send evil twin and infographic pitches with the live research as proof. |
| 9-12 | Guest posts go live (#5, #7). Publish interview (#8), pitch and record podcast (#9), publish the three posts (#10-12). Thank everyone. Log links earned. |

## Expectations

The source article's estimate: each hub earns roughly five to ten links; from a
low starting authority, about nine months of quarterly hubs before a
mid-difficulty commercial phrase ranks; leads flowing through the contact form
within a year. Treat these as the shape of the curve, not a promise. Higher
starting authority or a less contested phrase shortens it; a crowded phrase
lengthens it.

## What to measure

| Metric | Where | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Links earned per hub (referring domains) | Search Console links report or any SEO tool | End of quarter |
| Domain Authority | SEO tool | Quarterly |
| Sales page rank for the target phrase | Search Console, rank tracker | Monthly |
| Conversion rate by landing-page type (service vs blog) | GA4 segments | Monthly |
| Articles sent by sales to prospects | Ask sales; CRM if tracked | Monthly |
| Subscribers from the content upgrade | Email tool | Monthly |

## Assembling the hub with the user

Fill the twelve slots from the decisions recorded in steps 1-7. Name each
piece with a working title. State its purpose and what it links to. Place it on
the calendar. Then read it back: does every piece connect to at least one other?
Does anything exist only because it "felt like a blog post"? Cut or rewire it.
